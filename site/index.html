How to read this: "Edge" is how many points the model disagrees with the market line. Edges under ~2 points are noise. The Top 5 ranks bets by expected value per unit staked, assuming -110 pricing on spreads/totals and best available moneyline prices. Model methodology is in MODEL.md.

Data: CollegeFootballData.com. Ratings: SP+ (Bill Connelly/ESPN), FPI (ESPN), Elo, SRS. Lines shown are for reference only and move constantly — always confirm at the book. No model beats closing lines consistently; bet responsibly and only what you can afford to lose.
